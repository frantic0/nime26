Contact

Our preferred way to get your questions answered is to post on the NIME forum where the committee members (and others from community) can crowd source answers directly.

  • General inquiries can be sent to NIME 2026 general chairs Andrew McPherson and Courtney Reed at info@nime2026.org.

  • Questions about accessibility can be sent to the general chairs and to the NIME 2026 accessibility chairs Hugh Aynsley and Mathew Dalgleish at accessibility@nime2026.org.

  • Once the CMT portal opens in December 2025, if you have a question related to your NIME submission, you can send an email to all General Chairs by clicking “Contact Chairs” in the bar at the top of the screen.

  • You can contact your track chairs by clicking “Email Track Chair” shown in the track field for your submission, or by using the following addresses:
    • Paper chairs at papers@nime2026.org
    • Music and Installation chairs at music@nime2026.org or installations@nime2026.org
    • Workshop chairs at mailto:workshops@nime2026.org
    • alt.nime chairs at alt-nime@nime2026.org
    • Student Consortium chairs at student-consortium@nime2026.org
  • Feedback or questions on this website can be sent to NIME 2026 web chairs Francisco Bernardo and June Kuhn at web@nime2026.org.
